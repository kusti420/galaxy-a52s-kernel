## Not resolved / needs checking

- `battery,health_condition` is not read by the port; replaced by the derived
  `battery,health_condition_cycle/_asoc` pair (label only).
- `battery,prepare_ta_delay` (0) is not read by the port. No effect at 0.
- `pca9468,ta-mode = <1>` is not read by the ported PCA9468 driver; effect unknown. The direct
  charger must be validated on a USB power meter with a 25 W PPS charger before it is trusted.
- I2C aliases i2c21/i2c23 are new; they keep the sysfs device paths the ROM's sepolicy names.
- `pm7325_sdm_skin_therm` (Qualcomm reference channel on 0x146) must not be carried into the
  a52sxq tree; the phone uses 0x146 for `ap_therm`.
- r00/r01 overlays (hw rev 0/1) differ in the thermistor tables; not covered (rev 3/4 only).
- Properties the port reads that the phone's DT does not have take the donor's defaults. Not
  reviewed one by one.
