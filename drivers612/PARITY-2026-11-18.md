# a52sxq parity audit: 5.4 (ROM kernel) vs 6.12 real boot (rb31)

2026-11-18, OFFLINE ONLY (no phone touched, nothing built, nothing loaded). All four axes are
filled in; open points are marked "needs phone check" with the command.

Question: what does the phone's 5.4 kernel provide that the 6.12 real-boot build does not?
(The 51-bug audit of the same day looked only at what IS in the port.)

`modinfo -F alias` of the 252 loaded modules and the built-in alias list

vmlinux (parent drivers read children by name), then against the 294 unloaded modules.

strings, **59 nodes whose only driver is an unloaded module**, 99 with no driver anywhere
(most are property-only: reserved memory, caches, idle states). Scripts:
`scratchpad/dt/classify.py`, `diff54.py` (session scratch, not kept).
